Section B — Passage-Based Questions

Questions 11–15

Passage:

Archaeological evidence suggests that human societies changed gradually over thousands of generations. Early communities depended largely on hunting, gathering and other forms of food collection. Stone tools became increasingly diverse. Later, in several regions, people began cultivating plants and domesticating animals. Settled communities developed, food could be stored, and craft activities became more specialised. Eventually, some settlements grew into cities with organised streets, buildings, drainage systems and long-distance trade networks.
