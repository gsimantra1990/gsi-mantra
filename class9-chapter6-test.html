Section A – Multiple Choice Questions

1. Which statement best explains the basic principle of democracy?

(a) Power is inherited by a ruling family

(b) Political authority ultimately comes from the people

(c) Military institutions control the government

(b) Citizens should have a genuine choice

(b) It ensures that everyone is subject to law

4. Which is an example of representative democracy?

7. Which of the following can weaken democratic functioning?

(a) Free elections

(b) Citizen participation

(c) Concentration of power without accountability

(d) Rule of law

8. In a parliamentary system, the government is primarily accountable to:

(a) The military

(b) The legislature

(c) Foreign governments

(d) Private companies

9. Which country is an example of a presidential system?

(a) India

(b) United States of America

(c) Canada

(d) United Kingdom

10. Which combination is most important for a healthy democracy?

(a) Elections, accountability and rights

(b) Military power and censorship
