Question 1
MCQ
Which of the following was a major impact of the First World War on India?
Correct Answer: B
The First World War led to a huge increase in defence expenditure. The British government also increased taxes and imposed other economic burdens on the Indian people.
Question 2
MCQ
During the First World War, forced recruitment of soldiers mainly affected which region?
Correct Answer: B
Punjab was particularly affected by forced recruitment during the First World War.
Question 3
MCQ
What was the basic idea behind Mahatma Gandhi's concept of Satyagraha?
Correct Answer: B
Satyagraha was based on the power of truth and non-violence. Gandhi believed that injustice could be resisted without physical violence.
Question 4
MCQ
According to Gandhi, what was an essential quality of a Satyagrahi?
Correct Answer: C
A Satyagrahi was expected to have faith in truth and non-violence and to remain peaceful while resisting injustice.
Question 5
MCQ
The Champaran Satyagraha was mainly related to which issue?
Correct Answer: B
The Champaran Satyagraha of 1917 was associated with the problems faced by peasants who were forced to cultivate indigo.
Question 6
MCQ
Under which system were peasants in Champaran forced to cultivate indigo?
Correct Answer: B
Under the Tinkathia system, peasants were compelled to cultivate indigo on a portion of their land.
Question 7
MCQ
What was the main cause of the Kheda Satyagraha?
Correct Answer: C
In Kheda, crop failure and poor harvests made it difficult for peasants to pay land revenue. They demanded revenue relief.
Question 8
MCQ
What was the principal demand of the peasants during the Kheda Satyagraha?
Correct Answer: B
The peasants demanded suspension or relief from land revenue collection because of crop failure.
Question 9
MCQ
The Ahmedabad Mill Strike was mainly associated with:
Correct Answer: B
The Ahmedabad Mill Strike of 1918 involved textile workers demanding better wages from mill owners.
Question 10
MCQ
Which method did Gandhi use during the Ahmedabad Mill Strike?
Correct Answer: B
Gandhi undertook a hunger strike to support the demands of the Ahmedabad textile workers.
Question 11
MCQ
What was one of the most controversial features of the Rowlatt Act?
Correct Answer: B
The Rowlatt Act gave the government extensive powers to repress political activities, including detention without trial.
Question 12
MCQ
Gandhi launched which movement against the Rowlatt Act?
Correct Answer: B
Gandhi organised the Rowlatt Satyagraha in 1919 to oppose the Rowlatt Act.
Question 13
MCQ
Which of the following statements about the Rowlatt Satyagraha is correct?
Correct Answer: B
The Rowlatt Satyagraha involved peaceful protests, hartals and demonstrations against the Rowlatt Act.
Question 14
MCQ
The Non-Cooperation Movement was formally launched in:
Correct Answer: C
The Non-Cooperation Movement was adopted by the Congress in 1920.
Question 15
MCQ
Which of the following was a programme of the Non-Cooperation Movement?
Correct Answer: A
The movement encouraged people to boycott government educational institutions, courts, councils and foreign goods.
Question 16
MCQ
The meaning of the term 'Swaraj' was:
Correct Answer: B
The idea of Swaraj could have different meanings for different social groups depending on their experiences and aspirations.
Question 17
MCQ
What was the immediate reason for the withdrawal of the Non-Cooperation Movement?
Correct Answer: C
Gandhi withdrew the Non-Cooperation Movement in 1922 after the violent Chauri Chaura incident.
Question 18
MCQ
Why was the Swaraj Party formed?
Correct Answer: B
The Swaraj Party was formed to enter legislative councils and challenge colonial policies from within the councils.
Question 19
MCQ
Who were among the important leaders associated with the Swaraj Party?
Correct Answer: A
C. R. Das and Motilal Nehru were among the principal leaders who formed the Swaraj Party.
Question 20
MCQ
What was the main strategy of the Swarajists?
Correct Answer: B
The Swarajists wanted to enter the legislative councils and use them to oppose and obstruct colonial policies.
Question 21
MCQ
The Simon Commission came to India in:
Correct Answer: D
The Simon Commission arrived in India in 1928. It had been appointed by the British government in 1927.
Question 22
MCQ
Why was the Simon Commission widely opposed in India?
Correct Answer: A
The Simon Commission was opposed because it consisted entirely of British members and had no Indian member.
Question 23
MCQ
Which slogan became famous during the protest against the Simon Commission?
Correct Answer: B
“Simon Go Back” became a well-known slogan during protests against the Simon Commission.
Question 24
Chronological Arrangement
Arrange the following events in the correct chronological order:

1. Champaran Satyagraha
2. Kheda Satyagraha
3. Ahmedabad Mill Strike
4. Rowlatt Satyagraha
Correct Answer: D
The correct order is Champaran (1917), Ahmedabad (1918), Kheda (1918), and Rowlatt Satyagraha (1919).
Question 25
MCQ
Which of the following pairs is correctly matched?
Correct Answer: B
The Kheda Satyagraha was associated with the demand for revenue relief after crop failure.
Question 26
Matching
Match Column I with Column II:

A. Champaran Satyagraha
B. Kheda Satyagraha
C. Ahmedabad Mill Strike
D. Rowlatt Satyagraha

1. Mill workers
2. Indigo cultivation
3. Revenue relief
4. Protest against a repressive law
Correct Answer: A
Champaran was related to indigo cultivation, Kheda to revenue relief, Ahmedabad to mill workers, and the Rowlatt Satyagraha to opposition to a repressive law.
Question 27
Matching
Match the following correctly:

A. Mahatma Gandhi
B. C. R. Das
C. Motilal Nehru
D. Simon Commission

1. Swaraj Party
2. Satyagraha
3. Swaraj Party
4. No Indian member
Correct Answer: A
Mahatma Gandhi was associated with Satyagraha, while C. R. Das and Motilal Nehru were important leaders of the Swaraj Party. The Simon Commission had no Indian member.
Question 28
Chronological Arrangement
Arrange the following events in the correct chronological order:

1. Champaran Satyagraha
2. Rowlatt Satyagraha
3. Non-Cooperation Movement
4. Arrival of the Simon Commission
Correct Answer: A
The correct order is Champaran Satyagraha (1917), Rowlatt Satyagraha (1919), Non-Cooperation Movement (1920), and Simon Commission arrival (1928).
Question 29
Chronological Arrangement
Arrange the following events chronologically:

1. Ahmedabad Mill Strike
2. Kheda Satyagraha
3. Champaran Satyagraha
4. Rowlatt Satyagraha
Correct Answer: B
Champaran took place in 1917, followed by the Ahmedabad Mill Strike and Kheda Satyagraha in 1918, and then the Rowlatt Satyagraha in 1919.
Question 30
Chronological Arrangement
Arrange the following events in the correct chronological order:

1. End of the First World War
2. Rowlatt Act
3. Non-Cooperation Movement
4. Formation of the Swaraj Party
5. Arrival of the Simon Commission
Correct Answer: A
The correct sequence is the end of the First World War (1918), Rowlatt Act (1919), Non-Cooperation Movement (1920), formation of the Swaraj Party (1923), and arrival of the Simon Commission (1928).
