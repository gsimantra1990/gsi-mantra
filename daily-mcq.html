📘 History — Nationalism in India

Class 10 • NCERT • Advanced Conceptual MCQs

📅 10 September 2026
Jallianwala Bagh • Rowlatt Satyagraha • Non-Cooperation Movement
📝 MCQ 01 History
With reference to the events following the Jallianwala Bagh incident and the beginning of the Non-Cooperation-Khilafat Movement, consider the following statements:

1. General Dyer ordered firing at Jallianwala Bagh with the intention of creating terror and awe.

2. Gandhi called off the Rowlatt Satyagraha after violence spread in different parts of the country.

3. Gandhi considered the Khilafat issue an opportunity to bring Hindus and Muslims closer.

4. Non-Cooperation immediately began with full-scale civil disobedience.

Which of the statements given above are correct?
✅ Correct Answer: A — 1, 2 and 3 only
Statement 1 is correct because General Dyer ordered firing at Jallianwala Bagh and later justified his action as producing a moral effect.

Statement 2 is correct. Gandhi called off the Rowlatt Satyagraha after violence spread in several areas.

Statement 3 is correct. Gandhi saw the Khilafat issue as an opportunity for Hindu-Muslim unity.

Statement 4 is incorrect. Non-Cooperation was planned in stages. Full civil disobedience was not the immediate first step.
🎯 EXAM POINT

Jallianwala Bagh → Rowlatt Satyagraha withdrawn → Khilafat Issue → Hindu-Muslim Unity → Non-Cooperation
📝 MCQ 02 History
With reference to the Non-Cooperation Movement in towns, consider the following statements:

1. The movement initially received significant participation from the urban middle classes.

2. The Justice Party in Madras supported the boycott of council elections.

3. The boycott of foreign cloth contributed to a decline in imports between 1921 and 1922.

4. The movement gradually slowed partly because khadi was often more expensive than mill cloth.

Which of the statements are correct?
✅ Correct Answer: A — 1, 3 and 4 only
The urban middle classes initially participated actively. Students left government schools and colleges, teachers resigned and lawyers gave up legal practices.

The Justice Party did not support the boycott of council elections. It believed that participation could help it gain political power.

Foreign cloth imports declined substantially during the movement. Khadi was often expensive compared with mass-produced mill cloth, which created difficulties for poorer sections.
🎯 EXAM POINT Students → Schools boycott
Teachers → Resignation
Lawyers → Legal practice boycott
Foreign cloth → Boycott
Khadi → Symbol of nationalism

Ancient Indian History • Class 9/Competitive Level

🏺 MCQ 01 Harappan
Which of the following features most strongly indicates the highly organised nature of Harappan urban planning?
✅ Correct Answer: B
Harappan cities were characterised by planned streets, standardised brick sizes and an advanced drainage system. The drainage network is one of the strongest indicators of their organised urban life.
🎯 EXAM POINT Harappan Civilisation is particularly known for:

✔ Planned cities
✔ Drainage system
✔ Standardised bricks
✔ Craft production
🏺 MCQ 02 Harappan
Consider the following pairs:

1. Harappa — Ravi River
2. Mohenjodaro — Indus River
3. Lothal — Gujarat
4. Kalibangan — Rajasthan

Which of the pairs given above are correctly matched?
✅ Correct Answer: C — All four
Harappa was located near the Ravi, Mohenjodaro near the Indus, Lothal in Gujarat and Kalibangan in present-day Rajasthan.
🎯 EXAM POINT Harappa → Ravi
Mohenjodaro → Indus
Lothal → Gujarat
Kalibangan → Rajasthan
🏺 MCQ 03 Harappan
Which one of the following statements about Harappan script is correct?
✅ Correct Answer: A
The Harappan script has been found on seals, pottery and other objects, but it has not yet been conclusively deciphered. Therefore, many aspects of Harappan political and social life remain subjects of scholarly interpretation.
🎯 EXAM POINT 📌 Harappan script = Undeciphered
📌 Seals are important archaeological sources.

🕉️ Vedic Civilisation

Early Vedic • Later Vedic • Society • Economy • Polity

📅 Daily Advanced Practice
Rigveda • Society • Economy • Political Institutions
🕉️ MCQ 01 Vedic Civilisation
Which of the following is considered the earliest Vedic text and an important source for understanding the Early Vedic period?
✅ Correct Answer: B — Rigveda
The Rigveda is the earliest of the four Vedas and is a major source for understanding the Early Vedic period. It contains hymns dedicated to various deities and provides information about society, polity, economy and religious practices.
🎯 EXAM POINT Rigveda → Earliest Veda
Rigvedic society → Primarily pastoral
Important political institutions → Sabha and Samiti
🕉️ MCQ 02 Vedic Civilisation
Consider the following statements regarding the Early Vedic economy:

1. Cattle were an important measure of wealth.

2. Pastoralism played an important role.

3. Iron technology dominated the economy.

Which of the statements given above are correct?
✅ Correct Answer: C — 1 and 2 only
Cattle were an important form of wealth in the Early Vedic society. Pastoralism was also important. The statement regarding iron is incorrect because widespread iron technology belongs to a later phase and should not be used to characterise the Early Vedic economy.
🎯 EXAM POINT Early Vedic Economy:

🐄 Cattle wealth
🐎 Pastoral activities
🌾 Agriculture gradually gained importance
🕉️ MCQ 03 Vedic Civilisation
Which pair correctly represents important political assemblies of the Early Vedic period?
✅ Correct Answer: A — Sabha and Samiti
Sabha and Samiti were important assemblies associated with Early Vedic political life. They indicate that political decision-making was not entirely concentrated in the hands of the ruler.
🎯 EXAM POINT Early Vedic polity:

👑 Rajan
🏛️ Sabha
🏛️ Samiti

These institutions are important for understanding the political structure of the Early Vedic period.

🏛️ MCQ 01 Political Science
Which of the following best explains why power sharing is considered desirable in a democracy?
✅ Correct Answer: C
Power sharing reduces the possibility of conflict among different social groups and strengthens political stability. It is also a basic spirit of democratic participation.
🎯 EXAM POINT Power sharing → Conflict reduction → Political stability → Stronger democracy
