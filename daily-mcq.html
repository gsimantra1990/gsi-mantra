📝 DAILY ADVANCED MCQ – 01 History
What did Mahatma Gandhi mean when he described Satyagraha as “active resistance” rather than “passive resistance”?
✅ Correct Answer: C
Gandhi rejected the idea that Satyagraha was merely passive resistance. A Satyagrahi actively resisted injustice, but without physical violence or hatred.

The struggle was based on truth, soul-force, love and non-violence. Its purpose was not to inflict pain or destroy the adversary, but to appeal to the opponent's conscience and bring about change through moral force.
🎯 EXAM POINT

Satyagraha = Active Resistance + Truth + Soul-force + Non-violence + No ill-will
