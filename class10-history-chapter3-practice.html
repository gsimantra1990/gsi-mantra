📖 Chapter Overview

The Making of a Global World explains the historical process through which different parts of the world became increasingly connected through trade, migration, technology and the movement of capital.

The chapter discusses the pre-modern global world, the nineteenth-century economy, colonialism, migration, food and technology, and the impact of the two World Wars and the Great Depression.

📚 Important Topics

1. The Pre-modern World

Early trade, travel, migration and cultural connections between different regions.

2. Silk Routes Link the World

Trade and cultural connections through the historical Silk Routes.

3. Food Travels

The movement of food crops and their importance in shaping global societies.

4. Conquest, Disease and Trade

European expansion and its impact on societies, economies and populations.

5. Nineteenth-Century Global Economy

Trade, migration, labour and capital flows during the nineteenth century.

6. Corn Laws

The repeal of Corn Laws and its effect on international food trade.

7. Indentured Labour

Indian and other workers who migrated under indentured labour systems.

8. Technology and Globalisation

Railways, steamships, canals and communication technologies transformed global connections.

9. The Great Depression

The worldwide economic crisis of the 1930s and its impact on agriculture, trade and employment.

10. Post-war Reconstruction

The Bretton Woods system and the emergence of a new international economic order.
