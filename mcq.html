Topic: Satyagraha & Nationalism in India

📅 Date 06 September 2026

🎯 Level Advanced
⏱️ Mode Online Test
Q1. Satyagraha
In the context of Mahatma Gandhi's quote on Satyagraha (Source A), how is Satyagraha fundamentally distinguished from 'passive resistance'?
Correct Answer: (B)
Gandhi regarded Satyagraha as an active force based on truth and non-violence, rather than merely a method of passive opposition.
Q2. Chronology
Arrange the following historical events in their correct chronological sequence:

1. Satyagraha movement among cotton mill workers in Ahmedabad.
2. Peasants' struggle against the oppressive plantation system in Champaran.
3. Satyagraha in support of the peasants of the Kheda district.
Correct Answer: (C)
Champaran Satyagraha – 1917 → Kheda Satyagraha – 1918 → Ahmedabad Mill Strike – 1918.
Q3. Kheda Satyagraha
Which of the following factors primarily drove the peasants of Kheda (Gujarat) to demand a relaxation in revenue collection in 1918?

1. A sudden and severe industrial recession in urban centres.
2. Widespread crop failure in the agricultural sector.
3. The outbreak of a devastating plague epidemic in the region.

Select the correct answer using the code given below:
Correct Answer: (B)
Kheda peasants faced crop failure and the impact of plague. The industrial recession was not the primary cause of the Kheda revenue issue.
Q4. Satyagrahi Methodology
What is the core operational methodology of a true Satyagrahi when confronting an oppressor?
Correct Answer: (B)
Satyagraha seeks to transform the relationship between the opponent and the Satyagrahi through truth, non-violence and moral persuasion.
Q5. Growth of Nationalism
Consider the following statements regarding the growth of Nationalism:

(1) In Europe, nationalism was closely connected to the formation of nation-states.

(2) In colonies like India, the growth of nationalism was intimately connected to the anti-colonial movement.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
Correct Answer: (C)
Both statements correctly describe important aspects of nationalism in Europe and colonial India.
Q6. Assertion & Reason
Assertion (A): In India, different social groups responded to the call of nationalism with unified goals and identical experiences.

Reason (R): The shared oppression under colonial rule provided a common bond that tied many different groups together.

Choose the correct option:
Correct Answer: (D)
The Assertion is false because different social groups did not have identical experiences or identical goals. The Reason is true because colonial oppression created a broad common bond.
Q7. Economic Impact of World War I
Which of the following economic changes happened in India due to World War I?

1. Income tax was introduced.
2. Customs duties were lowered to help poor people.
3. Prices of daily items doubled between 1913 and 1918.

Choose the correct option:
Correct Answer: (C)
The war led to increased taxes, including income tax, and prices of essential goods approximately doubled between 1913 and 1918. The statement about lowering customs duties is incorrect.
Q8. Assertion & Reason
Assertion (A): There was widespread anger in the villages of India during World War I.

Reason (R): The British colonial government forced village youths to join the army and fight the war.

Choose the correct option:
Correct Answer: (A)
Wartime forced recruitment created serious resentment in rural areas, contributing to widespread anger during World War I.
